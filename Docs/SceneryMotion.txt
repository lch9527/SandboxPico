SCENERY ANIMATION — LEVELS 1–5

All levels: pennants sway; pines, round-canopy trees, ferns, ivy and daisies
move in the wind. Lupins sway where present. Existing river, waterfall and
cloud shader animation remains in place.
Level2: life rings swing and mooring ropes flex with anchored endpoints.
Level3: unpowered display wheels remain stationary.
Levels4 and 5: unused winch drums and cranks remain stationary.
Level5: all three Freight_Windmill rotors turn once every 15 seconds.

Ground clusters (Meadow verge, Mooring supplies, Workshop supplies, Quarry
scree and Freight garden) use Ground palette with zero displacement. Only
the distant Horizon transition islands use Horizon palette with explicit
0.2 metre float. The shader defaults to zero displacement for new materials.
The shared material previously made 17 mixed ground clusters bob, including
their rocks, plants and rigid props.

SceneryMotionRepair corrects saved scenes, published campaign prefabs and
reusable scenery prefabs without rebuilding meshes. Its triangle audit checks
pennants through their sway range and rotors through a full revolution,
excluding their attachment to their own mast or axle. Pedestal relocation
requires support under its corners. Repair-report.txt under Logs/SceneryMotion
lists corrected paths and explicitly identifies any unresolved intersections.
This geometry audit uses neighboring meshes at their rest pose; moving foliage
against other moving foliage and shader-only cloud intersections still require
visual review. Do not interpret a clean mechanical audit as exhaustive wind
collision validation.

Preview: MetaHackthone > Design Mode > Preview Scenery Motion.
Preview is temporary and stops before saving/export, Play, scene changes,
selection changes, Undo/Redo, script reload, or a click in the Scene view.
Stop preview before editing a transform in the Inspector. Save normally with
Ctrl+S outside Play mode to update the gameplay copy.

Select Environment and use Scenery Wind Settings to adjust Strength (0–1)
and Speed (0.25–2). The authored maximum strength keeps the baked motion
bounds valid. These controls affect vegetation and flexible ropes.
Mechanical parts and pennants use Car Scenery Breeze: Degrees Per Second
controls continuous rotation, Sway Degrees controls oscillation, Frequency
is radians/second, and Phase offsets the cycle. Keep the material pieces of
one wheel or winch on matching settings so they remain aligned.

Vegetation and rope animation runs in the GPU shader. Mesh UV channel 3
contains anchor/amplitude/frequency/phase; channel 4 explicitly contains rope
metadata or zero for plants. Rendering, shadows and depth use the same
deformation. No per-frame vegetation scripts or material instantiation.
The runtime shader clock is initialized once; level wind settings apply on
enable. The preview supplies a reversible editor clock.

Mechanical moving pieces were split once from existing mesh triangles,
preserving their rest surface and materials. Rope subdivision adds vertices
on the existing surface. SceneryMotionAuthored is a conversion stamp: it
never regenerates anything. Manually deleted moving parts stay deleted.
Do not rerun the old scenery dressing/build commands to apply these effects.

Reusable animated copies are under Assets/CreekDiorama/AnimatedScenery/Prefabs.
Original source prefabs were retained to avoid unexpectedly updating nested
instances and duplicating the newly authored moving parts.
Scene and campaign backups are under Logs/SceneryMotion/Before.
Validation captures are in Logs/SceneryMotion and Logs/CarScenery.

The new shaders derive from the installed URP Lit shader using
Tools/CreekDiorama/build_scenery_shader.py. Run this explicitly after a URP
upgrade and repeat shader/rendering tests; package sources are never edited.

This change does not install a new Quest build. Headset performance must be
checked after the next build/install; desktop tests are not a Quest benchmark.
