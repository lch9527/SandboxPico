Scope of Whole-level-totals.json
Read-only asset inventory, 2026-11-17.
Before: enabled, active, non-EditorOnly static MeshRenderers, selecting LOD0 from native LODGroups instead of adding all their alternatives.
After: replace catalog-supported foliage and display-base renderers with their LOD1 meshes; all other objects retain the same geometry as Before.
Not a Quest per-frame draw count. No frustum/occlusion culling, runtime vehicles, stereo multiplier or extra render passes included.
The generated foliage system currently has LOD0 and LOD1 only. Its last transition is zero, so it has no additional distant foliage tier or distance-cull threshold. Some unrelated props have their own LOD2.
The water-surface counts are only the Living Water shader mesh, not contact foam/other water effects.
Current Level4 excludes 2256 triangles belonging to native prop LOD1/LOD2 alternatives that are serialized enabled, avoiding simultaneous counting of multiple tiers.
