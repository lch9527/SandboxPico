Editing the five campaign levels

Open Level1.unity through Level5.unity from MetaHackthone > Design Mode.
Edit objects beneath Editable building layout: Building Area contains the
workbench/building artwork; Sandbox Level contains the course and scenery.
Move, rotate, resize, add, disable or delete the actual objects and colliders.
Keep new scenery beneath this level hierarchy so it is included in the game.

Save with Ctrl+S outside Play mode. Saving these five scenes automatically
updates their campaign prefabs. The existing Save Level N to Game commands
remain available. Check the Console if export reports a validation error;
the scene can save even when the game snapshot fails validation.
The headset needs a new APK build/install to receive subsequent saved edits.

Authored Objects is enabled on all five SandboxFactoryLevel components.
Track and puzzle generation, automatic start binding, boundary artwork
fitting/recreation, and legacy scenery batching do not replace authored
objects. Do not disable Authored Objects unless deliberately returning to
procedural authoring: a subsequent rebuild can replace hand-edited geometry.

The spline/sample components remain as gameplay route metadata. They do not
generate or reshape roads in authored mode. For a major route change, keep
the route guidance, start, goal and test bounds consistent with the physical
road; moving artwork alone does not redesign gameplay guidance. Moving a
whole track carries its saved samples with it.

Gameplay still animates obstacles, spawns playable construction parts from
their authored spawn markers, and runs effects. Edit spawn markers for part
placement; their editor preview children are disposable previews. Edits made
while Unity is in Play mode follow Unity's normal temporary-play rules.

Conversion backups, including the open Level5 edits, are in
Logs/ManualAuthoring. Regression tests are AuthoredCampaignTests.
